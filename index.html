Medical Image Segmentation

Medical image segmentation with deep learning

Deep learning approaches for medical image segmentation across U-Net, U-Net++, Attention U-Net, DeepLabV3+, TransUNet, Swin-UNet, and SegFormer, with hyperparameter optimisation tested against Dice score and IoU.
